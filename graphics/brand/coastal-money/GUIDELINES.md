# Coastal Money — Brand Guidelines

Personal brand of **Kyle Melvin**, Branch Manager and Sr. Loan Officer, Orange County, CA. Instagram **@mylenderguy** · NMLS #1486450. Covers social media, email marketing and general non-listing content.

Fonts are in `fonts/`, portraits in `img/`. Install or load the fonts before designing; do not substitute.

---

## 1. Two non-negotiables

1. **Personal brand, not the employer's.** Never show the Rev Mortgage name, logo or identity. When a client review names the employer, replace the name with "…" (e.g. "I worked with Kyle … for my FHA loan").
2. **Modern California editorial, not a mortgage infographic.** Big numbers, oversized type, short statements, restrained copy, lots of empty space. If it starts to look like a lender's rate flyer, start over.

**There is no logo.** Where a mark would go, set "Coastal Money" or "Kyle Melvin" in Sora Bold. Don't draw one.

## 3. Typography

| Font | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sora** Bold (700, 800 for story quotes) | Headlines, oversized numerals, brand name | Tracking −0.02em headlines, −0.03em numerals at 0.82 line height |
| **Manrope** 400/500/700 | Body, labels, fine print | Body line height 1.5, copy blocks kept narrow (about 22 characters wide) |
| **Caveat** 600 | A handwritten note or a review quote | One note per slide. Never on a hook |

- Sentence case everywhere. Headlines are never all-caps.
- Eyebrow labels are uppercase Manrope Bold, tracked +0.14em (`YEAR ONE`, `THE BOTTOM LINE`, `CLIENT REVIEW`).
- Key sizes on a 1080px canvas: hook headline 104px, review headline 84–92px, body 34–38px, eyebrow 26px. Nothing on a post smaller than 22px.

**Signature device: the oversized numeral.** `01`, `02+`, `$0`, `DM`. One to three characters in huge Sora Bold, terracotta for the lead item and sage for the second. It's what makes a post recognizable at thumbnail size.

## 4. Layout and surfaces

- **Feed post:** 1080×1350 (4:5). **Story:** 1080×1920. Export at 1× or 2×.
- Margins: 64px sides, 72px top and bottom.
- Anchor content to the top and bottom edges with an intentional gap in the middle. Don't float everything in the vertical center, and don't cram a block above a dead half.
- Backgrounds are flat. Exceptions: photo hooks (cinematic photo under a gradient scrim), and a very faint warm glow in the top-right corner of sand slides.
- No textures, patterns or decorative gradients.
- Hairline rules only: 1px, sand at 20% on navy, navy at 16% on sand.
- **Corner radii:** 0 for slides and photos, 6px buttons, 16px framed photos, 20px cards, fully round for portrait crops and dots.
- **No shadows** on carousel slides. Only exception: the floating quote card on review posts (soft navy shadow).
- No frosted glass, no backdrop blur, no translucent panels.

**Never add carousel chrome:** no @mylenderguy watermark, no slide count, no "SWIPE →", no dots. Instagram supplies all of it.

**Icons:** none, deliberately. Only the `→` arrow on a hook's continuation line and CSS terracotta dots as bullets. **No emoji, ever**: not in graphics, captions or subject lines.

## 5. Imagery

- Warm, moody, editorial-graded Southern California: golden hour, navy and terracotta tones, a genuinely empty third of the frame for the headline.
- Never a real listing, never logos or signage. No people in scene imagery (client photos the client has approved are the exception, see 8c).
- Kyle's portraits are natural light and unfiltered, circle-cropped in sign-offs, and never on a hook.
- Background photos behind text are faded (sand wash or scrim) and may be softly blurred so text stays readable.

---

## 6. Voice and copy

- **First person, direct, no hedging.** Kyle speaks as himself. The reader is always "you." Example: *"Send me the address. I'll tell you if it still works."*
- Short sentences that land flat. Rhetorical questions only as a headline ("Who pays for it?").
- No exclamation marks in Kyle's own copy. Client reviews are quoted as written.
- Em dashes sparingly.
- **Length per slide:** headline 3 lines, body 2 sentences, list 3 items, handwritten note 1 phrase. More than that becomes two slides.
- **The ask is always a DM keyword:** *Send me "BUYDOWN" and I'll run your scenario — no cost, no obligation.* Keywords in use: CONV, VA, DSCR, WAIT, BUYDOWN.
- **Content pillars:** Mortgage IQ (education), OC Real Estate (local market), Beyond the Mortgage (personal/relationship), Life (human, non-mortgage).

---

## 7. The hook (slide one, and every single-image post)

- The job is to **stop the scroll, not explain**. Make a claim that's hard to ignore and hold back how it works.
- One statement in Sora Bold at 104px, or one oversized numeral. Nothing competes with it.
- **Two-tone headline:** line one in Warm Sand sets the expectation, line two in terracotta breaks it. One turn per hook.
- A small, muted Manrope continuation line at the bottom naming the payoff: "The 1–0 buydown, explained →".
- Must be readable at 40% size (feed thumbnail on a phone).
- Hooks fail when they have: a topic label for a headline ("Buydowns 101"), more than 3 lines, a handwritten note, a busy photo, or a compliance mistake.
- Photo hook **or** flat navy hook with a huge numeral. Never a big numeral over a photo.

## 8. Post formats in use

**a. Carousel (5 slides, 1080×1350)**
1. Hook (navy, photo or numeral)
2. Editorial (sand, the one light slide; handwritten note allowed)
3. Numerals (navy, `01` / `02+` rows)
4. Big number (navy, one oversized stat)
5. CTA (navy: DM keyword ask, hairline, Kyle's sign-off with portrait and NMLS, disclosure)

**b. Client review post (1080×1350)**
Faded, softly blurred interior photo under a sand wash. Top: "in their words…" (terracotta, Sora Bold 92px), short terracotta-soft rule, "working with kyle" (navy, Sora Bold 92px), then ★★★★★ in terracotta. Middle: sand card, 20px radius, soft shadow, large terracotta-soft quote marks, the review in navy Caveat, short rule, then a sage `CLIENT REVIEW` eyebrow. Long reviews: trim before shrinking the type.

**c. Client photo post (1080×1350)**
Full terracotta field with a 48px border, client photo inside it with 16px rounded corners. No text on the image. Short caption (if any) in Sora Bold.

**d. Review story (1080×1920)**
Full terracotta field. Open navy frame drawn as two rounded L-shaped strokes (9px, round caps). Large navy Sora quote marks at the top-left and bottom-right corners of the frame. Review centered in white Sora ExtraBold (about 45–58px, tight 1.24 line height), with a "P.S." line on its own. Navy ★★★★★ above the closing quote mark. Trim the review to about 60 words.

**e. Email (640px wide)**
Same palette and type. Buttons: terracotta, 6px radius, sand text. A barely-there shadow is allowed on web/email only.

## 9. Compliance

- **Never state a note rate** in posted content. Dollar savings only.
- **Broker sign-off** before anything mentioning rates, programs, payments or tax posts. That includes reviews with claims like "best rates."
- Reg Z trigger terms (payment + rate + term + down payment together) require full disclosure.
- Any carousel naming a dollar figure ends with: *"Estimates only. Subject to underwriting approval. Not a rate lock or commitment. Equal Housing Opportunity."* plus Kyle Melvin and NMLS #1486450.
- Client reviews: unpaid, shared with permission, and checked with the broker before posting.

---

## 10. Don'ts

- Rev Mortgage name or logo anywhere
- A drawn logo
- Emoji
- Carousel chrome (watermark, slide count, swipe cue, dots)
- Rounded cards with colored left-border accents, or a "fintech app" card look
- Sage as a fill or background
- Shadows on carousel slides
- Icons or icon sets
- Note rates in posted content
- Substitute fonts
