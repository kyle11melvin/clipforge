# 2026-10-23 — The "IQ Quiz" reel: first full-screen cutaway

Kyle uploaded `IQ Quiz` (.MOV, 95 MB, 4K24 portrait, already SDR BT.709) to the Clipforge Drive folder and a
screenshot of his quiz landing page ("What's Your Financial IQ?", piggy-bank illustration). The ask: "make
this into a graphic that comes up on the screen, maybe even its own screen, so the camera's not just on my
face the entire time."

## Script (from the transcript, 16.6 s after trim)

"Hey there, it's Kyle your lender guy. Quick question for you. Do you know your financial IQ? Most people
don't and they wait until pre-approval to find out, and oftentimes it's too late. I put together a 15
question quiz that will tell you exactly your score and where you stand. Comment IQ below and I'll send it
over to you."

## What was built

- `clipforge build … --caption-y 0.19 --caption-style rounded` (the standing reel setup). The trim removed
  about a second of lead-in; transcript times are in cut time.
- `graphics/iq-quiz/comp.html`: a full-frame sand slide. Eyebrow "Free quiz", two-tone headline "What's
  your / financial IQ?", the real page screenshot (`quiz-hero.png`, 2000x744) sliding up in a 16 px-radius
  frame, Caveat "where do you stand?", bottom line "Free · Comment IQ →". Cut in full-screen 9.4–14.4 s
  while he describes the quiz. I had added two numeral rows ("15 questions", "IQ comment it below"); Kyle
  sent a crop of them and said "good but get rid of this part". Rows gone.
- `graphics/iq-quiz/cta.html`: a 1080x640 transparent overlay, "Comment **IQ**" (Sora, sand + terracotta,
  soft navy shadow), pip at the top 14.4 s to the end while he says "Comment IQ below". Readable over the
  wood-slat wall; the eyebrow "Want the quiz?" is faint there.
- `src/broll.mjs`: a cue may now carry its own `graphics` file and `pip` box, so one pass mixes a full-frame
  composition with a small alpha overlay. `src/graphics/render.mjs`: a single-time `--probe` no longer
  fails (hstack needed two inputs).
- Cover: `graphics/iq-quiz/cover.html` from the 20-down template, headline "What's your / financial IQ?",
  frame 2 of a numbered 12-frame strip (`ffmpeg select+drawtext '%{n}'+tile`) sent to him to choose from.
  Note: `sed` with `&` in the replacement garbled the headline once ("WhatYou don't need…"); edit cover
  text with python, not sed.
- Delivery page: https://claude.ai/artifact/SSP5nXKVPrcbq2mxppNQ27 (video, stills, cover + strip, caption
  slot). The phone file is 4.9 MB at crf 23 (indoor footage compresses well).

- Cover, take two: Kyle sent a separate smiling selfie against the same wall (a phone screenshot with
  black bars, cropped with PIL to the photo, then to 9:16 keeping the top) and asked for the hook above it:
  "what's your financial IQ or do you know your financial IQ". Used "Do you know your / financial IQ?",
  which is what he says on camera. So: he may supply a still for the cover instead of a frame.

- "Should we move the text above my head since there's a lot of empty space above me": the two-line 98 px
  headline left a gap. Went to three lines at 132 px from y=190, filling the wall above him.
