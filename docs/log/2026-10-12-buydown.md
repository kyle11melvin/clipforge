# 2026-10-12 — "Make year one cheaper": the 1-0 buydown Reel, carousel and story frame

The brief came in fully specified (scene by scene, with the math worked out and the styles labelled the
way the Coastal Money Project writes handoffs). No footage; all five scenes are full-screen graphics.

## What was built

`graphics/buydown-cm/comp.html` → `videos/buydown-cm/`:

- `coastal-money-buydown.mp4` — 28 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, no audio.
- `carousel/slide-1.png` … `slide-5.png` — the same five scenes at 1080x1350, animation landed.
- `story-cta.png` — the separate 1080x1920 story frame with an empty band for the link sticker.
- `contact-sheet.png` — one still per scene.

Scenes: hook "Payment too high at 7.40%? / Make year one cheaper." (sand then terracotta after a beat,
continuation line "The 1-0 buydown, in dollars →"); sand setup ($1,000,000 home, 20% down, 7.40% from
Freddie Mac PMMS Oct 8 2026, Caveat note "Now cut year one by 1%."); 01/02 rows (with a 1-0 buydown
$5,004 in year one; without, $5,539); "$6,420" counting up with "Back in your pocket while you settle
in.", "$535 a month for twelve months." and the rule of thumb "about $67 a month for every $100,000 you
borrow" held for the last 3.4 s; DM sign-off (BUYDOWN keyword, hairline, portrait, name only).

## The math (verified in the session)

$800,000 loan, 30-year fixed, P&I only. 7.40% → $5,539.04/mo. 6.40% → $5,004.05/mo. Difference
$534.99/mo, $6,419.89 in year one, $66.87 per $100,000 borrowed. Shown as $535, $6,420 and "about $67".

## Calls made without asking

- The brief put the story sticker zone at about y 1250–1550 and the sign-off below it but above the
  bottom 340 px, which leaves 30 px. The zone was moved up to y 1100–1400 (still 1080x300 of empty navy)
  so the hairline, portrait and name fit at y 1424–1580.
- Per the brief, no NMLS, disclosure, company name or URL anywhere. This is the first Coastal Money cut
  without the sign-off line; the WAIT post still carries it.
- Instagram safe zones: every line of type sits between y 250 and y 1580 on the Reel. The carousel
  slides use the guide's 72 px margins instead. The hook headline moves up slightly on the carousel.
- The sand slide's faint warm glow from the WAIT cut was dropped, since the brief says no gradients.

## Pipeline change

`src/graphics/render.mjs`: `--stills <dir>` keeps every probe still at full size (that is how the
carousel PNGs and the story frame are exported), `--mode <name>` loads `comp.html#<name>` so one
composition can switch layouts (`carousel` repositions to 1080x1350, `story` shows the CTA frame), and
a single-still probe no longer fails in FFmpeg.
