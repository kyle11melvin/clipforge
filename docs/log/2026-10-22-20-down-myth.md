# 2026-10-22 — The "20% down myth" reel: first real talking head with cards

Kyle shot a 31 s walking selfie in downtown Carlsbad ("Hey guys, it's Kyle your lender guy…") and uploaded
it to the Clipforge folder on Google Drive as `20% down myth .MOV`. He asked for the usual captions and
"some numbers": "20% down" with an X through it, then "3%".

## What happened

- **Drive worked this time.** The Google Drive connector listed the file, but its download tool caps at
  10 MB and the clip was 265 MB. The file had link sharing on ("anyone with the link"), so
  `https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=<id>&export=download&confirm=t` fetched it through the
  proxy. Rule for next time: look for the file by name, then curl that URL. If it 403s, the file isn't
  link-shared; ask him to attach it in chat.
- **iPhone footage is HDR.** The .MOV was 4K60 HEVC HLG (BT.2020). HyperFrames saw that and promoted the
  render to HDR, trying to pre-extract 11 GB of raw frames; the render stalled. Fix in `src/ffmpeg.mjs`:
  `probe()` reports `hdr`, and `assemble()` tone-maps HDR sources to SDR BT.709 (`zscale` + `tonemap=hable`)
  after the downscale, writes a keyframe every second (HyperFrames wants this for exact seeks) and tags the
  output BT.709. Hable looked the most natural on a bright sunlit street.
- **Transcript fix.** Parakeet heard "2%" for "20%". Edited `transcript.json` and re-ran (the cut and
  transcript are reused, so the fix is one step).
- **Cards above his head.** The graphics are a landscape 1080×640 composition
  (`graphics/20-down-myth/comp.html`, Coastal Money colours, Sora/Manrope/Caveat) rendered with
  `--size 1080x640`, then cut in as picture-in-picture at the top of the frame (`pip: {width: 0.8, y: 48}`)
  so they sit in the sky above his head and never touch the captions at 70%. `src/broll.mjs` now takes
  `pip.border` and `pip.borderColor` (4 px sand here instead of the Rev gold).
- **Three cues** from the transcript: "you don't need 20% down" (12.85 s; the X slams through 20% at
  14.0 s, right after he says it), "as little as 3% down" (18.35 s), "seller credit… buy your rate down"
  (26.3 s). A fourth DM / name / NMLS card was built and **Kyle asked for it to be removed** from the video
  as soon as he saw the stills. The scene is still in the composition (seconds 10.5–13.5) but not cued.
- **Delivery.** Artifact page with the player, Save MP4 (`downloads`), the contact sheet, a copyable
  caption. The posted file is a 17 MB re-encode (crf 23, 4.2 Mb/s cap) because artifact assets cap at
  20 MB; `final-broll.mp4` is the full-quality master (89 MB) in `videos/` (gitignored).

- **Captions moved off his face.** The 30%-from-bottom default put the caption on his chin in this framing.
  Added `--caption-y` to `clipforge build` and re-rendered at 0.19; captions now sit on his chest, above
  Instagram's own caption overlay. Kyle: "Move the text so it's not on my face."

- **"Can we see what it looks like without the full cards?"** Built a second version where the type floats
  over the footage with no navy panel. `src/graphics/render.mjs --alpha` screenshots with a transparent
  background and encodes ProRes 4444 (`.mov`); `broll` honours the alpha (pip with `border: 0`). The
  composition reads `#nocard` from the URL fragment (`comp.html#nocard`) to drop its background and add a
  soft navy text shadow; the sage eyebrow turns sand there because sage is a label colour on navy only.
  Both versions are on the artifact page; Kyle picks.
