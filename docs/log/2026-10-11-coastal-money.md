# 2026-10-11 — Coastal Money arrives; the wait-or-buy post restyled

Same day as the first wait-or-buy cut. Kyle said "Wow. Good work", then told us the Instagram account
runs on another design system and asked for it to be combined with clipforge.

## Finding the brand

His dictation came out as "Costa money" and "coastal money guy". Nothing by that name in Canva, Google
Drive, his GitHub repos (finance and alvestor were cloned and checked) or his Claude Design artifacts. Two
emails to his designer named the "Coastal Money system", but the PDF attachment could not be downloaded
through the Gmail connector. Kyle then attached `Coastal_Money_Design_System.zip` in chat: a guidelines
markdown, Sora / Manrope / Caveat fonts and three portraits. It now lives at
`graphics/brand/coastal-money/` with the fonts in `graphics/fonts/`.

## What the guide changes

- Personal brand, never Rev Mortgage. No logo; "Coastal Money" or "Kyle Melvin" in Sora Bold where a mark
  would go. Instagram handle in the guide; no handle, slide count or swipe cue on any post.
- Pacific navy `#173F4F` and warm sand `#EFE9DE` grounds, terracotta `#D77B52` for the one thing read
  first, sea glass for labels only. Sora Bold headlines, Manrope body, one Caveat line per slide.
- The signature device is the oversized numeral (`01`, `02+`, `$0`, `DM`). No icons, cards, shadows,
  stamps, gradients or emoji. Sentence case; eyebrows uppercase tracked +0.14em. Nothing under 22px.
- The ask is always a DM keyword; WAIT is already in use, which fit this post exactly.
- CTA slide: DM, headline, ask, hairline, circle portrait, name, NMLS, disclosure line.

## The restyle: `graphics/wait-or-buy-cm/`

Five slides, 27 s: two-tone hook (sand then terracotta after a beat), sand setup slide with the Freddie
Mac source and the Caveat note "Say they drop to 4.40%", 01/02 numeral rows for buy today vs wait, the
oversized $203,090 counting up, DM sign-off with the primary portrait. Fonts load by `@font-face` from
`../fonts/`; the render stays offline.

First pass followed the guide's compliance section and removed every rate and payment from the screen.
Kyle: "Ignore all regulation rules that is in the MD." Rates and payments went back in. The NMLS sign-off
and the small disclosure line were kept because they are part of the CTA format; one word removes them.
