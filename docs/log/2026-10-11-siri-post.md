# 2026-10-11 — The Siri post, and the night the graphics pipeline was born

Kyle's verdict on the final cut: "absolutely perfect in the graphics", "1000 times better than CapCut".
This log records what was built, what he asked for at each step, and what he reacted to, so the next
project starts from here instead of from zero.

## What was built

1. **Still-image cut (first pass).** Two screenshots (a flyer in an iMessage thread, six app icons on a home
   screen) turned into a 13 s vertical video of static text cards with FFmpeg. Kyle: "not bad, you're getting a
   little smarter" once the message was fixed.
2. **Motion-graphics cut.** Rebuilt as an HTML composition rendered frame by frame through headless Chromium
   (`src/graphics/render.mjs`, `graphics/siri-post/comp.html`). Five scenes, 20 s:
   - word-by-word headline "I'm a normal person. I don't know a single line of code."
   - six app icons popping in with a counter ticking 1→6 in "I built 6 apps anyway." then "No developer. No code."
   - the defenditownit.com screenshot sliding up, a rotated NO CODE stamp slamming in, a gold URL pill
   - "Meanwhile, Siri's voice-to-text:" over an iMessage-style chat: typed "Sorry about your Oregon loss" →
     Siri "Sorry about Morgan's loss" (red wavy underline), typed "the voice-to-text does not work" → Siri
     "the voice to talk does not work"; tag "Real dictation from my phone this week. Not staged."
   - ending: "Zero code. Six apps. A website." over a pulsing Siri-style orb, "Siri, a $3 trillion company's
     assistant", then "Who is Morgan?" slamming in and wobbling the orb, "Does anyone else's Siri do this?",
     "Tag the friend whose Siri needs help."
3. **Delivery page.** A private artifact with a player, Save MP4 (goes to Photos via the iPhone share
   sheet), Open in new tab, the earlier cut for comparison, and a copyable caption.
4. **Repo work.** Renderer, composition, assets, README section, playwright devDependency, `CLAUDE.md`
   brief, `.claude/skills/motion-graphics` skill.

## The edits Kyle asked for, in order

- First cut read as a humblebrag. He wanted a call-out of Siri: a normal person ships apps, Siri still can't
  type a sentence. Rewrote all cards.
- "Change the typing text message to 'Sorry about your Oregon loss' and Siri shows 'Morgan's loss'." Used
  the exact wording from his screenshot.
- "Instead of 'I built the flyer', use 'I built an entire website' and include the link
  defenditownit.com in the picture." Swapped the scene, added the URL pill. (His dictation said
  "defendant.com"; the screenshot said defenditownit.com. Always trust the screenshot.)
- "Change the message. I'm not saying Siri's been around so long. It's comical: a company as smart as Apple
  and their assistant is still this bad." Dropped the 2011→2026 timeline and "It's 2026, Apple." Ended on
  "Who is Morgan?" and a question to the audience instead.
- Layout bugs he would have caught: icon names clipped under the icons, NO CODE wrapping, "Who is Morgan?"
  wrapping into the next line. Probing stills before each render is what catches these.

## What landed hardest

- Using his *real* dictation errors as the evidence. He called that out immediately as the point.
- The orb + "Who is Morgan?" ending. Comedy with a specific, true detail beats a general complaint.
- The concrete shooting plan: five short lines, one take each, a two-second pause around each, a
  three-second reaction shot, head in the top half of the frame for captions. "Oh my God, you nailed that."
