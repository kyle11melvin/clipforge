# 2026-11-28 — VA county loan limit 2027 (walking talking head, lender account)

Same day as stroller math, after that one was approved and merged (PR #7). Kyle: "Let's do another one about the VA
county loan limit increasing, let's give the new number for San Diego and Orange County."

## Numbers

- FHFA announced the 2027 limits on 2026-11-24, effective 2027-01-01. One-unit: Orange County $1,283,100 (2026:
  $1,249,125, up $33,975), San Diego County $1,143,100 (2026: $1,104,000, up $39,100). Baseline $855,400. VA county
  limits follow FHFA's. Found by web search across several outlets that agree; FHFA's own county table was not opened
  from the session, and the page says so.
- The point the script adds: a veteran with full entitlement has no loan limit (since 2020); the county figure only
  caps zero-down buying with partial entitlement (VA Lenders Handbook, ch. 3). With a $400,000 VA loan still out in
  Orange County, the zero-down price goes from $849,125 to $883,100, the same $33,975 as the limit.
- Written as a lender talking to veterans. He is not a veteran.
