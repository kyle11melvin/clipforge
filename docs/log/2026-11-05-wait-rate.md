## Picking the lines

for real speech boundaries (Parakeet stretches a word's end to the next word's start, so its ends are useless
for cutting; its starts run ~0.3 s early on soft consonants).

- Take 2 is the clean full take: line 1 exactly as scripted, line 2 "I was talking to a friend that's looking at

  and 4 clean ("fifty thousand dollars in seller credits or price cuts").
- Take 1 for the closer: "If you're waiting on rates, what actual rate are you waiting on?" ("actual" is the

- Take 3 is a 7 s pickup of line 3 only; take 4 has "cut that part" and a stumble on line 5; take 5 has an "uh".
- **He never says "send me WAIT" on camera.** All takes end on the question. The DM card and the caption carry

  loudnorm), with an optional per-cut `gain` dB (added to `assemble` in `src/ffmpeg.mjs`). Then

- `videos/wait-rate-office/`: EDL with the pauses and the "um" cut (five cuts, 17.6 s), captions above his head
  at `--caption-y 0.66`, no cards (the take has no number to put on one). Cover from the 2.3 s frame.
- Parakeet dropped "with one" from "the phone with one of my good friends"; patched into `transcript.json`
  before the render. Check captions against what he says, not just numbers.
- **"Why does my face look orange?"** The HDR→SDR chain in `src/ffmpeg.mjs` (`npl=100`, hable, `desat=0`)
  blew out HLG skin under warm indoor light. Compared six settings on one frame: `npl=300`, mobius, `desat=2`
  gives natural skin at the right exposure (1000 nits is too dark, 100 too bright and orange). Changed for
  every future HDR clip. Earlier HDR reels (20-down-myth) were outdoors, where it was less visible.
- The office cut on the same page, so he can compare.
- "Still looks orange" after the tone-map change. The room light is warm; the SDR conversion alone leaves his
  skin orange. Tried white-balance and saturation corrections on one frame: `colortemperature=temperature=7200,
  eq=saturation=0.85` (higher temperature = cooler in that filter; 8000 goes purple, 5000 goes warmer) gives
  neutral skin with the wood still warm. Added `grade` to the EDL (per cut or top-level, any ffmpeg filter
  chain after the tone-map) so this stays per shot rather than baked into the pipeline.
