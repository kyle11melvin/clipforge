# 2026-11-05 — "What rate are you waiting for?": the first multi-take talking head

Brief from the Coastal Money Project: a 25 s talking-head reel, pillar "Beat the rate", keyword WAIT, five
scripted lines plus a 3 s silent reaction shot, pip cards for lines 1, 2 and 5, full-screen cards for 3 and 4,
no NMLS / phone / email / disclosure anywhere. Footage: five takes in the Drive folder `Clipforge / Waiting for
rates to drop`, uploaded the same morning (`Nov 5, 2026 at 11-31-53 AM.mov` … `11-35-27`), 25–37 MB each,
fetched with the `drive.usercontent.google.com` curl trick. Walking selfie outdoors in a shopping plaza,
1080x1920 after rotation, SDR BT.709 (no tone-map needed), head top around y 550–650, chin around 1300.

## Picking the lines

Transcribed every take on its own (`npx hyperframes transcribe <take> -d <dir> --json`), then `silencedetect`
for real speech boundaries (Parakeet stretches a word's end to the next word's start, so its ends are useless
for cutting; its starts run ~0.3 s early on soft consonants).

- Take 2 is the clean full take: line 1 exactly as scripted, line 2 "I was talking to a friend that's looking at
  buying this year and I asked him that question and he didn't have a number", then a natural **3.0 s** silence
  looking at the camera (the only take with the full beat; take 1 has 2.3 s, take 4 2.2 s, take 5 1 s), lines 3
  and 4 clean ("fifty thousand dollars in seller credits or price cuts").
- Take 1 for the closer: "If you're waiting on rates, what actual rate are you waiting on?" ("actual" is the
  punch; take 2 says "rates to drop, what rate"). Take 1 is 0.8 LU louder, so that cut carries `gain: -0.8`.
- Take 3 is a 7 s pickup of line 3 only; take 4 has "cut that part" and a stumble on line 5; take 5 has an "uh".
- **He never says "send me WAIT" on camera.** All takes end on the question. The DM card and the caption carry
  the keyword; told him.

## What was built

- `src/edl.mjs` (new): `node src/edl.mjs videos/<post>/edl.json` assembles the best delivery of each line across
  takes into `cut.mp4` through the same `assemble()` as `clipforge build` (scale, 30 fps, keyframe per second,
  loudnorm), with an optional per-cut `gain` dB (added to `assemble` in `src/ffmpeg.mjs`). Then
  `clipforge build <anything> --name <post>` reuses `cut.mp4` and goes straight to captions and the render.
  `videos/wait-rate/edl.json` is the record of the picks. 20.6 s from 5 cuts.
- `clipforge build … --caption-style rounded --caption-y 0.20 --caption-hide 9.2-12.4,14.1-17.2`.

  "My friend / Great income. Great credit. Down payment saved. / *No number.*"; navy DM card "Waiting on rates?
