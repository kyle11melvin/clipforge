Kyle's brief: a 20–30 s Instagram Reel from three new clips in his Drive "Clipforge" folder
(IMG_8217, IMG_8223, IMG_8230), driving to his home buyer quiz. Hook in the first two seconds, best
take, dead air out, original audio at -14 LUFS, large captions inside the Reels safe zones, no NMLS,

clip and timestamps were used, and ffprobe the final.

## Getting the footage in (most of the session went here)

- The Google Drive connector could only see files Claude itself created. Kyle disconnected and
  reconnected Google Drive in the Claude app, ticking every box on Google's permission screen, and the
  folder appeared. Keep this in CLAUDE.md: it is the fix for "folder not found".
- The connector refuses downloads over 10 MB, and phone clips are 47–64 MB. The working route is the
  public share link fetched with curl, which needs `drive.google.com` and `drive.usercontent.google.com`
  on the environment's Allowed domains (Network access → Custom). Kyle added them from his Mac.
- Transcription needs the Parakeet model from Hugging Face. That needs `huggingface.co` **and**
  `*.hf.co` allowed, and even then HyperFrames' own downloader (Node fetch) ignores the proxy. Fetch
  the four model files with curl into `~/.cache/hyperframes/parakeet/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-int8/`
  and `npx hyperframes models install parakeet` then verifies them.
- GitHub web upload was attempted as a fallback: the upload page is hidden on the phone layout, and the
  browser limit is 25 MB per file, so it is not a route for phone video.
- Kyle wants the shortest possible instructions, one tap per line, and got angry at long replies.

## Pipeline lessons

- Three 4K 60 fps HEVC inputs in one FFmpeg filter graph got the process OOM-killed (14 GB). Shrink
  each clip to 1080x1920 30 fps first, one at a time, then assemble.
- iPhone footage is HLG HDR. Left alone, HyperFrames renders HEVC. Tone-map to SDR BT.709 at the
  intermediate stage (`zscale … tonemap=hable …`) and render with `--sdr`; the picture is warmer and
  the output is H.264. Compared frames: the SDR version looked better, not worse.
- `assemble` now normalises to -14 LUFS and resamples to 48 kHz (it was -16 and left AAC at 96 kHz).
  The HyperFrames render lowered loudness by about 1 dB; remux the normalised audio onto the rendered
  video with `-c:v copy` and check again.
- Transcribe each clip separately for clean per-clip word times, then cut by hand. The silence trim is
  fine but the slate talk ("Okay, ready, action", "Ready go") is speech, so it needs the transcript.
- In a HyperFrames composition, a timed `.clip` nested inside another timed `.clip` runs on the parent's
  clock: the CTA words vanished until the wrapper lost its `clip` class. Snapshot with
  `npx hyperframes snapshot <dir> --at t1,t2` before a six-minute render.
- Artifact pages cap supporting files at 15 MB, so the Save MP4 button serves a 5 Mbps copy
  (final-ig.mp4, 13 MB) and the CRF 16 master goes through chat as an attachment.
